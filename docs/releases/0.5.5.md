# 0.5.5: Versioned Tables in the 0.7.7 Preview

*2026-11-25 · every FLUX document stays valid · the 0.7.7 preview is re-pinned*

0.5.5 moves the FLUID 0.7.7 preview to a newer forge-cli commit. The FLUX
schema only widens its compatibility window, so every FLUX document that
validated under 0.5.4 still does. A contract that declares FLUID 0.7.7 is
checked against the new pin, which accepts the conventions of a versioned
table and more, and refuses three forms the old pin accepted.

## What a 0.7.7 contract may now carry

- On the end of a validity window (the dimension with `isEnd`): `openEnd`,
  the value an open version's end holds (`null`, or a literal of the end
  column's type such as `9999-12-31`), and `chain`, how a closed version's
  end meets the same entity's next start (`nextStart`, `dayBefore` or
  `none`).
- On the natural entity: a `lifecycle`, with its `activation` column, an
  `endMarker` (the value a final version holds) and a `currentFlag` (the value
  the last version holds).
- On a relationship: `asOf`, a DATE or TIMESTAMP column of the expose, so the
  relationship reaches the version valid on it.
- `alerts` for the DAGs forge-cli generates, an Iceberg table's
  `maintenance`, a Spark build on `runtime: local` or from a prebuilt
  `package`, a stream's `deadLetter` and `maxOffsetsPerTrigger`, an Airflow
  pool per output table, and Airflow DAG and task ids in Airflow's own
  spelling, capitals, dots and dashes included.

## What it refuses now

These validated under 0.5.4:

| Contract | Write instead |
|---|---|
| an Airflow `dagId` or `taskId` longer than 250 characters | an id of at most 250 characters |
| an Airflow `dagId` or `taskId` that ends with a line break | the id without it |
| `runtime: emr-eks` with a `releaseLabel` of the form `emr-X.Y.Z` | the image's label: `emr-X.Y.Z-latest` or `emr-X.Y.Z-YYYYMMDD` |

## Upgrading

Validate your bundles with 0.5.5 before you rely on it: each refused form is
named with its path in the contract. `verify` checks a pack's 0.7.7 contracts
against the preview the running release carries, so a pack that 0.5.4 made
from such a contract fails under 0.5.5 with `bundle no longer validates`.
Verify that pack with flux-spec 0.5.4. To get a pack that 0.5.5 verifies, fix
the contract and make a new pack, which needs a new signature. Contracts on the
FLUID releases 0.7.3 to 0.7.6, the enforcement contract and the bundle manifest
are unchanged.
